FAQ

Cleaning in Werribee — common questions

What areas does LNS Maintenance Services cover?
We cover Werribee (3030), Point Cook (3030), Hoppers Crossing (3029), Tarneit (3029), Truganina (3029), Williams Landing (3027), Wyndham Vale (3024) and Manor Lakes (3024) across Melbourne's west, plus the regional cities of Geelong (3220) and Ballarat (3350). See all service areas →
Do you guarantee I'll get my bond back after an end of lease clean?
We offer a bond-back re-clean guarantee: if your property manager raises a cleaning concern at the final inspection, tell us within 72 hours with the agent's written feedback and we return to re-clean the affected areas free of charge. We clean to the standard of a typical Victorian exit condition report.
How fast can you come?
We operate 7 days a week, 7am–11pm. Regular house, carpet and office cleans are often available same or next day; for end of lease cleans we recommend booking 3–5 days ahead to secure your slot.
Can you clean under the NDIS, and how does invoicing work?
Yes — we clean for NDIS participants whose plans include household cleaning under Assistance with Daily Life. We provide itemised invoices with description, date, hours and rate for self-managed or plan-managed participants, and can coordinate with your support coordinator.
How much does cleaning cost in Werribee?
Pricing is fixed-price and depends on property size, condition and inclusions. As a guide, a 2-bed unit bond clean runs about $250–$380; a 4-bed house with carpets, oven and windows about $480–$650. You're never charged more than the agreed quote.
Do you bring your own products and equipment?
Yes — our team arrives fully equipped with all solutions, cloths, mops, vacuums and steam gear. You don't need to provide anything; tell us if you'd prefer a specific product.
Are your cleaning products safe for children, pets and sensitivities?
We use eco-friendly, low-toxicity products as standard and can work with fragrance-free, low-VOC formulations for households with children, pets, allergies or NDIS/aged-care needs. Mention sensitivities when booking.
What's the difference between a deep clean and an end of lease clean?
A deep clean refreshes a home you're staying in (ovens, grout, behind furniture, fans). An end of lease clean follows your agent's exit checklist and carries our bond-back guarantee. Not moving but want a full reset? Choose a deep clean.
How often should I have my home professionally cleaned?
Families with kids or pets usually suit fortnightly cleaning; singles or couples often monthly. We also recommend a full deep clean every 6–12 months. Weekly, fortnightly, monthly and one-off options with no lock-in for residential.
Do you offer carpet steam cleaning, and how long does it take to dry?
Yes — hot-water extraction that lifts stains, dirt, allergens and pet odour. Most residential carpets are walkable within 2–4 hours.
Are your cleaners vetted and insured?
Our cleaners are vetted before working with us and we carry public liability insurance. Body corporates, facility managers and NDIS plan managers can request our certificate of currency at info@lnsmaintenanceservices.com.au.
How do I get a quote?
Call 0424 120 509 or email info@lnsmaintenanceservices.com.au — quotes are free, fixed-price and usually returned within one business day.
